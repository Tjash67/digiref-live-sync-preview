DIGIREF · FOOTBALL PILOT

Set up DigiRef on your iPhone

No computer is needed for a short camera test. This is the web pilot, not an App Store or TestFlight app.

Open DigiRef

Add it to your Home Screen

  1. Open DigiRef using the button above in Safari, not an embedded social-media browser.
  2. Tap Safari’s Share button, then Add to Home Screen. If shown, enable Open as Web App, then tap Add.
  3. Launch the DigiRef icon and allow camera access when prompted. Use this same icon for future tests; Safari and the installed app may keep separate local recordings.

Your first test: 30 seconds

  1. Open Camera, select the rear camera, and mount the phone sideways with the field visible.
  2. Under Record & review, name the test and tap Start recording.
  3. Wait 15 seconds, tap MARK, then wait another 10–15 seconds.
  4. Tap Stop & save → Saved video → Open video & markers. Verify playback and download the video and evidence report.

Keep the app visible and the phone unlocked. DigiRef requests a screen wake lock when supported, but locking your phone, switching apps, or receiving interruptions can end capture. Check recording status before every play.

What does 50 MB mean?

At our requested 6 Mbps recording bitrate, the shared-upload limit holds about 70 seconds. Actual size varies; use 30–45-second tests for upload headroom.

The limit applies to shared uploads, not a timer that stops local recording. This pilot is not validated for full-game capture. Download important footage before clearing browser data.

Internet is needed to load the app and share footage. External account confirmation email is still awaiting configuration. AI suggestions are experimental; humans make all rulings.

Native iPhone capture remains a separate build requiring Mac/Xcode compilation and physical-device validation.
